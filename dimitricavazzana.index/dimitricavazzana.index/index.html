Dimitri Cavazzana is a professional golf coach and former competitive tour player based in South Tyrol, Italy. With over 20 years of experience in golf and 5 years competing on elite circuits — the Alps Tour, MENA Tour, Clutch Tour, and Ecco Tour — he brings first-hand knowledge of the sport at the highest level to every coaching session.

Dimitri offers private coaching across three main regions: Europe (Italy, France, Switzerland, and the Alps), the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, and the MENA region), and Asia. He also travels to other destinations on request — wherever the game takes you.

Dimitri's philosophy starts with listening. He believes technique must follow the athlete — not the other way around.
