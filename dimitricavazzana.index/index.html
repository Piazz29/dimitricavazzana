— FAQ

Common Questions

The Private Clinic is a 5-day full immersion program completely tailored to your game. It includes daily technical sessions covering long game, short game and putting, plus 9 or 18 assisted holes each day with Dimitri on the course. Course management, mental coaching and tour player methodology are integrated throughout every session.

The Group Clinic is designed for small groups of maximum 5–6 players. It combines professional-level technical coaching with on-course sessions, maintaining individual attention within the group dynamic. The format runs over 5 days in an exclusive location.

Clinics take place across Europe (including the South Tyrol Dolomites), the Middle East (Dubai and the Gulf), and Asia. Dimitri selects exclusive venues where he has established professional relationships, ensuring the highest standard of facilities and experience.

The clinics are designed for serious golfers who want measurable improvement — from committed amateurs looking to break their limits, to competitive players preparing for tournaments. The program is fully adapted to your current level and specific goals.

Use the contact form below or reach out directly by phone or email. Dimitri personally reviews every inquiry and will contact you to discuss the best clinic format, location and dates for your goals.

Dimitri brings tour player methodology to every clinic — not textbook instruction, but the real approach used by professional athletes on competitive circuits. Combined with exclusive locations and a maximum of 1 (private) or 6 (group) participants, the experience is incomparable to any standard golf school.
